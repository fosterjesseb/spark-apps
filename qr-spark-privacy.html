QR Spark: Scanner & Generator

Privacy Policy · Effective November 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Android app QR Spark: Scanner & Generator (package com.fosterjesseb.qr_spark, “the App”), published by Spark Apps (“we”, “us”), handles information. We built the App to work on your device and to collect as little as possible.

In short: we have no accounts and no servers. Your scans, the codes you create and your history stay on your phone. The only data that leaves your device is collected by Google AdMob, which shows the ads that keep the App free.

Information processed only on your device

  • Camera. The camera is used only to read QR codes and barcodes in real time. Camera images are processed on your device and are never stored or sent anywhere by us.
  • Images from your gallery. If you choose a picture to scan, it is read on your device only to find a code in it. We do not upload or keep it.
  • History. Codes you scan or create are saved in a local history on your device so you can find them again. You can clear the history at any time with the delete button on the History tab; uninstalling the App deletes it as well.
  • Saving and sharing. When you save a QR image, it is written to your device gallery. When you share a code, Android’s standard share menu is used, and you choose where it goes.

We (the developer) do not receive any of this information.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share with Google:

  • device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID;
  • approximate location, derived from your IP address;
  • app interactions, such as ad views and taps;
  • diagnostics and crash information.

This data is used for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention, and it is encrypted in transit. Google processes it under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device). Optional rewarded videos that unlock extra QR colors are also provided by AdMob and work the same way.

Permissions

  • Camera to scan codes.
  • Internet to load ads.
  • Storage (Android 9 and older only) to save QR images to your gallery.

Links in QR codes

Scanned codes may contain links to websites, phone numbers or email addresses. When you open one, it is handled by another app or website (for example your browser) whose own privacy policy applies. We are not responsible for third-party content.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data retention and deletion

We do not store your data on any server. Local history stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App. Data collected by Google AdMob is retained under Google’s policies linked above.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App changes. The new version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
